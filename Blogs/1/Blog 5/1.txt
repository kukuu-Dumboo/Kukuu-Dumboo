IF I COULD SHOW YOU YOURSELF THROUGH MY EYES

There is one thing I wish I could give you.

Not flowers.

Not jewellery.

Not some expensive gift.

Not even a huge surprise.

I wish I could somehow let you see yourself through my eyes for one day.

Just one day.

Because I don't think you understand what I see when I look at you.

You probably see your flaws first.

The things you want to change.

The days when you don't feel confident.

The mistakes you've made.

The parts of yourself you think aren't enough.

But that's not the person I see.

I see Siddhi.

And I don't mean the perfect imaginary version.

I mean the real one.

The one who gets angry.

The one who gets childish.

The one who laughs like Shinchan.

The one who suddenly gets excited while telling me some random tea.

The one who can irritate the living shit out of me and then somehow make me laugh five minutes later.

The one who says "mann nahi hai" again and again.

The one who can be confusing.

Emotional.

Stubborn.

Beautiful.

Annoying.

Kind.

All of it.

That is the person I fell in love with.

Not some perfect girl.

You.

Sometimes I think about the little girl you were before I ever existed in your life.

I wish I could have met her.

I wonder what she used to laugh about.

What scared her.

What she dreamed about.

What silly things made her excited.

What she thought her future would look like.

And if I could meet her, I think I would tell her one thing.

You're going to be okay.

You are going to grow.

You're going to have amazing days.

You're going to have horrible ones.

Some people will hurt you.

Some people will love you.

You will change in ways you can't imagine.

And one day, somehow, you are going to become the girl that a stupid boy named Eshan falls completely and ridiculously in love with.

I wish I could show that little girl the woman she becomes.

I think she would be proud.

And I am proud too.

Not only because of things you've achieved.

Not because you're beautiful.

Not because you're my girlfriend.

I'm proud because you keep going.

For every difficult day nobody saw.

For every moment you were tired and still continued.

For every time you wanted to give up but somehow made it through another day.

You don't have to be perfect.

Actually, I don't want you to be perfect.

You don't have to always be the strongest person in the room.

You can be confused.

You can cry.

You can get angry.

You can have a terrible day.

You can make mistakes.

You can start again.

You can change your mind.

You can be childish.

You can be completely yourself.

I don't want you to ever think you have to become somebody else to deserve love.

Because your existence itself matters.

Not because of what you do for me.

Not because you make me happy.

Not because you are beautiful.

Not because you are my girlfriend.

You matter because you are you.

That idea has become important to me because loving you changed something inside me too.

Before you, I used to think my purpose was mostly about building.

I still love that.

I still want to make crazy things.

I still want to work like hell.

I still want to build things that make people stop and ask how the hell I made them.

I still want to look at my past self and make him proud.

But now there is another part of my purpose.

I want to become a man who can protect what he loves.

A man who is stronger.

A man who can provide stability.

A man who takes care of himself.

A man who can stand beside the person he loves instead of becoming another problem she has to carry.

You didn't pull me away from my goals.

You showed me why some of them matter.

That is a difference I wish you would understand.

And I don't want us to stop growing.

I don't want us to be exactly the same ten years from now.

I want future us to look at current us and laugh.

I want older Siddhi to look at current Siddhi and say, what the hell was I doing?

I want older Eshan to look at current Eshan and say the same thing.

But I also want both of us to feel proud.

Not because we became perfect.

Because we kept becoming better.

That's the kind of future I imagine.

Not a perfect relationship.

A growing one.

Two idiots who fell for each other and somehow keep choosing each other while life keeps changing around them.

I think about how many versions of you I haven't met yet.

There is future Siddhi.

Older Siddhi.

Wiser Siddhi.

Crazier Siddhi.

The version of you who will discover things about herself that even you don't know yet.

And I want to meet all of them.

That is one of my favourite things about loving someone.

You don't just love the person who exists today.

You become curious about all the people they are going to become.

And I hope you get to watch me change too.

Maybe one day we'll be properly old.

Like actually old.

You'll still be annoying me.

I'll still pretend to be irritated.

You'll still laugh at me.

And we'll sit somewhere remembering how ridiculous we were when we were young.

Maybe we'll find these old writings.

Maybe you'll read them and laugh at how dramatic I was.

Maybe I'll be embarrassed by half of it.

But I hope one thing still survives all the years.

I hope you still understand what I was trying to say.

That I was once a boy who looked at a girl and thought,

God, I'm really lucky she exists.

And he never stopped thinking it.

Thank you.

Not just for loving me.

Thank you for existing.

For every laugh.

Every call.

Every stupid conversation.

Every movie.

Every game.

Every good morning.

Every goodnight.

Every fight we survived.

Every moment you made me smile without knowing it.

Every time you allowed me into your world.

Thank you for giving me memories I never thought I would have.

Thank you for changing an ordinary life without even trying to.

Sometimes I look at how ridiculously huge the universe is.

Billions of people.

So many cities.

So many lives.

So many completely different stories happening at the same time.

And somehow our paths crossed.

Out of all the random possibilities...

I found you.

I don't know how much of that was coincidence and how much was something else.

I don't even know if there is a word big enough for it.

But I know what it feels like.

It feels like I got lucky.

Really fucking lucky.

I have said "I love you" to you more times than I can count.

And somehow I still don't think it explains enough.

Maybe words were never designed for feelings like this.

You can write pages.

You can explain.

You can analyse.

And there is still something left that you cannot put into a sentence.

I think that's okay.

I'll keep trying anyway.

Because you're worth every word.

I want to be there for the tiny moments.

The important moments.

The stupid moments.

The boring moments.

The messy ones.

The days when you know exactly what you're doing.

The days when you have absolutely no idea.

I don't want only your best days.

I want your real life.

Because that's what loving someone actually means to me now.

Not possessing.

Not controlling.

Not finding someone perfect.

Just being there.

Protecting without restricting.

Supporting without changing who they are.

Growing together.

And when life gets difficult, reminding each other that one bad chapter isn't the whole story.

So go.

Live your life.

Grow.

Explore.

Try stupid things because they're fun.

Travel.

Laugh.

Chase the things that scare you.

Become whoever that little girl inside you wanted to become.

I will be proud of you.

And I hope somewhere in all of that beautiful life you create, there will still be a small place for your stupid Esh.

One day, maybe much later, you'll look back at everything.

And I hope you see thousands of memories.

People.

Places.

Dreams.

Wins.

Losses.

Everything.

And somewhere among all those memories, I hope you see a stupid boy who loved you very, very much.

The boy who met you on a random April night.

The boy who got addicted to your voice.

The boy who couldn't stop checking his phone for your messages.

The boy who said "I love you" near a Shiv temple and nearly cried.

The boy who got a purple octopus keychain and treated it like treasure.

The boy who waited through distance.

The boy who messed up, apologized, learned, and tried again.

The boy who wanted to become better because his heart had found someone it wanted to protect.

The boy who looked at you after all of that and still thought the same thing.

Yep.

It's her.

It's always been you.

And maybe, in the end, that's enough.

I love you, Siddhi.

Not only for what you are to me.

But for who you are.

Always.

Your Esh.
