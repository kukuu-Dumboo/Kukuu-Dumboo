THE NIGHT I WASN'T SUPPOSED TO MEET YOU

I still remember April 23rd, 2025.

It is weird how some dates just stay inside your head. You don't try to remember them, you don't write them down somewhere, you just remember. Maybe because something happened on that day that changed the direction of your life without asking for permission.

That day had been exhausting. There was all the chaos of the college fest, work, chores, everything that needed to be finished, and by the end of it I was lying on my bed like a dead man. I wanted to sleep. I really wanted to sleep.

Then my friend called.

They wanted me to join an Omegle session and talk to random strangers.

I didn't even like the idea. I was tired and I had absolutely no reason to go. But there was this stupid little feeling inside me saying, just go.

I don't know why.

I just followed it.

After a bunch of completely weird conversations with strangers, we came across a group of girls. Nothing about that beginning looked important. It was just another random conversation on another random night.

Then there was you.on 

We started talking normally. Then somehow the conversation kept going. We laughed, talked about ourselves, our past, random experiences, some flirting here and there, and just... talked.

For some reason I was drawn towards you.

That was strange for me because I had never been attracted to a girl just from talking for a few hours. My brain didn't know what to do with that feeling, so naturally I ignored it.

Eventually we exchanged Instagram.

You said you'd send me reels.

And in my head I had already decided what would happen.

This is never going anywhere.

She'll probably send one reel, maybe two, and then disappear like most random internet conversations do.

Boyy was I wrong.

The call ended.

Five minutes later, I got a reel from you.

I remember being genuinely shocked.

Still, even then, I didn't think much would come out of it.

Then the next day we started texting.

Texts became longer.

Longer texts became calls.

Calls became hours.

And somehow, without noticing when it happened, talking to you became part of my day.

A day started feeling weird if we didn't talk.

A day started feeling incomplete.

You used to tell me things about your day, your life, your past, random little things that probably wouldn't matter to anybody else, and I just loved listening.

Especially your voice.

I don't know how to explain it properly. It felt like a melody from some far away world that I couldn't reach. Somehow listening to you made my head quieter. It made some of the pain disappear. I could listen to you for what felt like an eternity and still want another hour.

My heart used to beat ridiculously fast while talking to you.

And saying goodbye actually started feeling sad.

Not because anything bad happened. Just because I didn't want the call to end.

I started waiting for your calls.

I started checking every notification hoping it was from you.

I started missing you before I had even fully understood why I missed you.

Things started changing slowly and slowly.

It was an alien feeling. I had never experienced anything like it before.

My life, which had felt like an aimless hustle for a long time, suddenly started feeling beautiful.

I wanted to know everything about you.

Your habits.

Your routine.

Your tone.

When you were happy.

When you were irritated.

What made you laugh.

What made you sad.

The more I knew, the more I wanted to know.

And then came the little things that somehow became huge things.

Our first good morning was just a few days after we met. I don't even remember who said it first anymore, but I remember how beautiful it felt.

Then you gave me my first nickname.

"Esh."

You gave it to me on May 1st.

It sounds like such a small thing, but I loved that name so much. I still do.

Then there was the first fight.

Honestly, I don't even remember what it was about.

And maybe that's the best part.

We fought, we survived it, and eventually the actual problem became so unimportant that I can't even remember it. I remember apologizing much more clearly than I remember the fight.

Then there was the first "I miss you."

You said it when I was coming back to Bangalore from Delhi.

We hadn't even met during that trip, and still, when I was going away, you felt the distance.

That one hit me.

Then the first time we made each other laugh uncontrollably happened when we actually met. You kept laughing and expressing yourself and somehow your laugh made me laugh too.

Then you gave me my first gift.

A purple octopus keychain and a Kinder Joy.

12th June, around 7:30 PM.

I still remember it.

I keep that keychain with me because sometimes an object stops being an object. It becomes a timestamp. It becomes a little piece of a person.

Then came your first photo.

You were at a Hanuman temple during my Manali trip. You took a selfie and sent it to me.

I still remember thinking it was one of the cutest things ever.

And then there was the biggest first of all.

6th May.

Around 6 PM.

I was sitting near the Shiv temple while talking to you.

And suddenly this huge feeling came over me.

I didn't want to say "I like you."

I didn't want to say "I have feelings for you."

I didn't even want some careful, logical sentence.

I wanted to say exactly what my heart was screaming.

"I love you."

I had never said those words to someone before.

I was almost crying.

And after I finally said it, I felt like I was on heaven.

It was beautiful.

Just like you.

Looking back now, I sometimes laugh at how little that night looked while it was happening.

It was just one tired guy who almost went to sleep instead of joining a stupid internet call.

One decision.

One random conversation.

One girl.

And suddenly an entire new chapter of my life existed.

That's the weirdest thing about life.

We spend so much time trying to control everything. We make plans, backup plans, long-term plans, short-term plans, and then something completely random happens and becomes more important than all of them.

I wasn't searching for you that night.

I wasn't prepared to meet you.

I didn't even think the conversation would continue for another day.

But somehow, it did.

And slowly, you stopped being a random girl I met online.

You became the person I wanted to tell everything to.

The person I wanted beside me during the stupid moments, not just the important ones.

The person whose name could completely change my mood when it appeared on my phone.

The person whose voice could bring me back from a horrible day.

The person who made me realize that sometimes the things we aren't looking for are exactly the things we needed.

Maybe that night was just luck.

Maybe it was intuition.

Maybe it was coincidence.

I don't know.

I only know one thing.

If someone had told me that night, while I was lying on my bed exhausted, that joining that random call would eventually lead me to the girl I would fall deeply in love with, I would've laughed at them.

But that's exactly what happened.

And that is how I met the love of my life for the first time.

Not in some movie.

Not through some grand romantic plan.

Just on an ordinary night when I almost went to sleep.

And somehow...

I found you.
