IT WAS NEVER THE BIG THINGS

I used to think memories were supposed to be big.

Trips.

Birthdays.

Important dates.

Grand surprises.

The kind of moments people put into videos with cinematic music.

But when I think about us, the memories that stay with me are usually stupidly small.

A voice on a phone.

A random reel.

Someone laughing.

A nickname.

A keychain.

A movie.

A "good morning."

A call that lasted much longer than it was supposed to.

Maybe that's what happens when someone becomes a part of your actual life.

The big moments are important, but the little things start collecting around them.

I still remember your smile.

I genuinely don't know how to describe it without sounding dramatic, but I have tried many times anyway.

Whenever you smile, I smile.

It feels like your happiness somehow gets transferred to me.

And your laugh is even worse.

You laugh like Shinchan.

There is something so childish and innocent in it that I can't help smiling when I hear it.

Sometimes I don't even care what the joke was.

I just like hearing you laugh.

Then there is your voice.

I have said this before and I will probably say it a thousand more times, but your voice is more soothing to my ears than music.

I can listen to music for hours, but after a while my ears get tired.

With you, somehow it is different.

You can just start telling me some random tea about your day with all that energy and excitement, and suddenly you're sounding like a little kid who found something extremely important that the entire world must know.

And I love that.

I love the way you tell me things.

I love the way you get excited.

I love the way you get irritated.

I even love the little words you use to ragebait me or shut me up.

Although let's be honest, getting you irritated is fun, but making you properly angry?

Don't try that at home.

That shit is scary asf.

There are so many things I notice about you without even trying.

Your habits.

The way your tone changes depending on how you're feeling.

What makes you happy.

What makes you quiet.

What kind of things you want to tell me immediately.

I started noticing all of it because I wanted to know you.

Not some interview version of you.

Not a perfect version.

Just you.

That's why I remember the firsts.

Our first good morning.

The first nickname.

The first fight.

The first apology.

The first "I miss you."

The first gift.

The first photo.

The first "I love you."

I don't remember every exact conversation.

I couldn't possibly.

But I remember the feeling attached to them.

That might actually be more important.

I remember "Esh."

You gave me that name on the morning of May 1st.

It was such a small thing and somehow it became one of my favourite names.

I remember the purple octopus keychain you gave me with a Kinder Joy on June 12th around 7:30 PM.

There was nothing luxurious about it.

But I love it because you gave it to me.

I remember the photo you sent from the Hanuman temple during my Manali trip.

You were just standing there, taking a selfie and sending it to me.

And I remember thinking, this is one of the cutest things I have seen.

I remember the first time I told you I love you.

May 6th.

Around 6 PM.

I was near the Shiv temple.

I had never said those words to someone before.

My brain was trying to understand what was happening while my heart had already made the decision.

When I finally said it, I was almost crying.

And then I felt ridiculously happy.

I also remember the completely unserious stuff.

Watching "5 Feet Apart" together.

That movie actually got to me.

It changed the way I looked at romantic movies because somehow I could feel it to my core.

Then "Me Before You."

All those movies have a different place in my heart, but not simply because they were good movies.

It was because of the person I watched them with.

That's something I didn't understand before.

An ordinary movie can become a memory when the right person is sitting on the other side of the call.

Same with games.

Among Us was probably one of the most fun things we did.

I loved every second of it.

Getting annoyed.

Laughing.

Trying to figure out who the imposter was.

Doing absolutely stupid things together.

And I still think Roblox is fun, by the way.

I don't understand why you hate it.

Maybe one day I'll convince you.

There are still so many movies we haven't watched.

So many games we haven't played.

So many anime episodes waiting for us.

So many places we haven't gone to.

And honestly that is one of my favourite things to think about.

We haven't even experienced most of our memories yet.

There are entire years of stupid conversations that haven't happened.

There are trips we haven't planned.

Foods we haven't tried.

Places we haven't seen.

Fights we haven't had.

Fights we haven't laughed about yet.

And maybe one day we will look back at these current memories and laugh at how young and ridiculous we were.

That idea makes me happy.

Because when someone becomes your person, you stop wanting only extraordinary days with them.

You want the boring ones too.

You want to sit beside them doing absolutely nothing.

You want to hear about their day.

You want to send them a stupid reel and wait for the reaction.

You want to tell them about some random invention you are building.

You want to wake up hearing their voice.

You want to say goodnight even when there is nothing left to talk about.

The little things become the relationship.

That's why some of my favourite memories with you are not even "events."

They are moments.

You waking me up.

You talking about something random.

You laughing.

You getting annoyed with me.

You suddenly becoming childish because you're excited.

You saying "chup baith."

Me pretending to be offended.

You telling me some tea.

Me wanting to know every little detail anyway.

These are the things that make you who you are.

And there are probably hundreds of things about you that I still haven't written down.

Maybe thousands.

Some feelings don't translate into words properly.

I can write pages and still know that what I actually feel is much bigger than what made it onto the screen.

Maybe that's why writing about you is so hard.

Every time I finish something, I think of ten more things I forgot.

And maybe that's okay.

Because I don't actually want our story to become complete.

I want it to keep adding little moments.

Another first.

Another memory.

Another random night.

Another movie.

Another laugh.

Another ridiculous argument.

Another good morning.

Another gift.

Another "I miss you."

Another "I love you."

And someday, hopefully, a whole lifetime of ordinary little things.

Because maybe that is what life actually is.

Not the huge moments we wait for.

Just millions of tiny moments that don't look important while they're happening.

And somehow one day you look back...

and realize those were the important ones all along.

So whenever I think about us, I don't just think about the big things.

I think about the little things.

Because those little things are the ones that slowly made a random girl from one random night become the person I call my favourite girl.

My baby.

My kukuu.

My Siddhi.

And my person.
