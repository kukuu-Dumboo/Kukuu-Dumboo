THE HARDEST PART IS THE DISTANCE

There is a strange cruelty in loving someone and not being able to reach them.

You can call.

You can text.

You can hear their voice.

You can see their face through a screen.

You can spend hours talking.

And still, at the end of the day, there is one stupid physical fact sitting between you.

They aren't here.

That is what makes long distance so weird.

You can feel incredibly close and incredibly far away at the exact same time.

When I found out that I wouldn't be able to meet you for the next one and a half years, it destroyed me more than I showed.

I was surrounded by people, so I couldn't react the way I wanted to.

I wanted to scream.

I wanted to shout.

I wanted to let all of it out.

But I couldn't.

And honestly, I don't think I can completely imagine what you went through hearing it either.

This wasn't exactly the chapter we planned.

Our relationship has had its share of problems.

Misunderstandings.

Arguments.

Distance.

Bad days.

Moments where one of us didn't know what the other was thinking.

And I'm not going to pretend that all of it was easy.

It wasn't.

Sometimes a fight would completely destroy my concentration.

I could have academics going on.

Projects.

Side gigs.

Portfolio building.

Health and fitness.

Everything.

And still my mind would get stuck on one thing.

Are we okay?

When you stopped talking to me during a fight, it hurt more than I knew how to explain.

Sometimes I got anxious.

Sometimes I cried.

Sometimes the pain stayed there until we finally talked again.

I wasn't used to caring that much about losing someone.

Before you, I was pretty good at telling myself not to give a fuck.

Something goes wrong?

Move on.

Problem?

Solve it.

Person leaves?

Life continues.

Then you came along and broke that whole system.

Suddenly I had someone whose silence could affect my entire day.

And that scared me.

Because it meant you mattered.

A lot.

I also know that I haven't always handled that fear correctly.

I have messed up.

There were times I raised my voice when I shouldn't have.

Times I forced my way of thinking onto you.

Times I tried to fix things so aggressively that I ended up hurting the person I was trying to protect.

That is one of the hardest things for me to admit.

My intention was never to hurt you.

But intention doesn't erase what actually happened.

You can love someone deeply and still hurt them.

You can want the best for them and still say the wrong thing.

You can be trying to protect someone and accidentally become a source of pain.

I learned that the hard way.

One of my biggest problems has always been that I want to fix things.

If there is a problem, I immediately want a solution.

When I care about someone, that instinct becomes even stronger.

So sometimes I would tell you things like eat properly, don't keep eating junk, go to classes, sleep on time, stop watching reels all night, take care of yourself.

In my head, I was trying to help.

But love is not just about having the right solution.

Sometimes the person you love doesn't need a solution.

They need someone to sit beside them and say, I'm here.

That is something I am still learning.

I don't want to be your manager.

I want to be your partner.

I want us to make each other better, but not by trying to redesign each other.

There is a difference.

And I think we started understanding that difference slowly.

We both had habits and beliefs that came from our own lives.

We both had things to learn.

We both messed up.

But somehow we kept coming back to each other.

That matters to me.

During all the previous fights, we came through because somewhere underneath all the anger was trust.

There was love.

There was the decision to continue.

That's why I believe distance cannot automatically destroy something.

Distance can test it.

Distance can expose every weak point.

Distance can make small misunderstandings feel massive.

But distance can also teach you how to communicate.

How to trust.

How to be patient.

How to miss someone without turning that missing into resentment.

And maybe that's what this chapter is for.

To make us stronger.

I don't know what exactly the next one and a half years will look like.

I know there will be difficult days.

There will be days when I desperately want to see you and can't.

There will be nights where a hug would solve something that a two-hour call can't.

There will be moments when I wish I could simply sit next to you.

But I also know something else.

I am willing to wait.

Not because waiting is easy.

Because you are worth waiting for.

I don't care whether it is one and a half years or longer.

The feeling I have for you doesn't come with an expiry date.

It has left a permanent mark on me.

And I don't say that because it sounds romantic.

I say it because that is genuinely how it feels.

There is a voice inside me that keeps saying, it will work.

Don't worry.

I don't hear that voice all the time.

But when I am completely certain about something, I know it.

And I am strangely certain about us.

Maybe that's crazy.

Maybe that's naive.

But I would rather be honest about what I feel than pretend everything is perfectly predictable.

We have already survived things I never thought I'd experience in a relationship.

We have had moments where we both wondered what the hell was happening.

And still we are here.

Still talking.

Still laughing.

Still irritating each other.

Still loving each other.

That's not nothing.

Sometimes you tell me you don't want to burden me by sharing your problems.

I need you to understand something.

Your problems have never been the burden.

Not even a tiny fraction of one.

If you are sad, talk to me.

If you want to yap, yap.

If you need help, I'm here.

If you can't concentrate on studying, call me.

If you want to fall asleep peacefully, I'm here.

If life feels too heavy for a while and you just need somewhere to escape to, come to me.

I want to be that person.

Not the person who only gets your best days.

The person who gets the real ones too.

And I want you to know the same applies to me.

I don't always need to be the strong one.

Sometimes I need kindness too.

Sometimes I want someone to ask how I am.

Sometimes I want sweet words.

Sometimes I want to feel cared for without first having a crisis that justifies it.

We both deserve that.

Because a relationship shouldn't become another stressful thing sitting on top of an already stressful life.

It should be the place where you can breathe.

The world can be brutal enough.

I don't want us to make it harder for each other.

I want us to become the opposite.

A place where we can put everything down for a while.

A place where both of us can grow.

A place where we can say, I messed up.

I'm sorry.

Let's fix it.

And then actually move forward.

That is the kind of relationship I want.

Not perfect.

Real.

Distance doesn't make it easy.

But maybe easy was never the point.

Maybe some things become more valuable because you had to fight for them.

So yes, this is a difficult chapter.

We are going to miss each other.

We are going to have bad days.

We are going to misunderstand things sometimes.

But I don't believe those things automatically mean the story ends.

I think they mean we have another problem to solve.

And I know what my answer is.

If we have each other, we can cross the obstacle.

If the world gives us a difficult problem, then fine.

We'll solve it.

One day, after all these calls and texts and screens, I want there to be a simple moment.

No network problems.

No countdown.

No "I wish you were here."

Just you standing in front of me.

And me finally getting to hold you.

Until then...

I'll wait.

Patiently.

And I'll keep saying the same stupid thing in my head.

IT WILL WORK.

DON'T WORRY.

Esh isn't going anywhere.
